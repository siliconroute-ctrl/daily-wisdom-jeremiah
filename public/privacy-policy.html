Privacy Policy

Daily Wisdom from Jeremiah

Who We Are

Daily Wisdom from Jeremiah is operated by Inspired Living with Joao de Gois, based in Paarl, South Africa, under Silicon Route. We are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

For questions or requests, contact us through our Facebook page: facebook.com/rcmass

What We Collect and Why

We collect only what is strictly necessary to operate the app:

  • Email address — to create and secure your account
  • Subscription date — to build your personal verse archive
  • Notification preference — to respect your communication choices
  • Device token (FCM) — only if you opt in to push notifications, to deliver the daily verse to your device

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, browsing history, or any sensitive personal information. We never sell or share your information with any third party for commercial purposes.

Consent and Notifications

Daily notifications are off by default. They are only sent if you actively switch them on in Settings — this is your explicit consent under POPIA. You can withdraw consent at any time by switching the toggle off in Settings, and delivery stops immediately. Your device token is removed from our system when you opt out.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored securely on Google Firebase servers, protected by industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. These servers may be located outside South Africa. Such transfers are permitted under Section 72 of POPIA because Google provides adequate protection through binding corporate rules and data processing agreements.

Your Rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent for notifications at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via our Facebook page and we will respond within a reasonable time.

International Users

Our community spans Nigeria, West Africa, Brazil and South Africa. This policy is designed to comply with POPIA (South Africa), NDPR (Nigeria), and LGPD (Brazil). The same opt-in principles apply to all users regardless of location.

Children

This app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
